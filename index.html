About

Lindokuhle Mgoduka

I am a two-time Cum Laude graduate with a strong academic record, including a 90%+ pass mark in Software Development I. I have a solid understanding of programming fundamentals and a passion for helping other students succeed.


Over the past few years, I have worked as an SI Leader, Residence Tutor, and Peer-to-Peer Tutor, supporting many first-year students in challenging modules such as ONT1030/SDS1010. I understand the difficulties students face because I have helped many of them overcome the same struggles.


My teaching approach focuses on simplifying complex concepts, using real-life examples, and guiding students step by step until they truly understand — not just memorize. I believe every student can succeed in programming when given the right support and explanation.


Through this platform, part of Small Empire Innovations (SEI), my goal is to create a supportive and student-friendly learning environment where you can build confidence, improve your skills, and successfully pass your IT modules.
