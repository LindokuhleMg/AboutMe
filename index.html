About SEI Tutoring

A student-focused tutoring platform designed to help IT students gain confidence and improve academically through practical, understandable teaching.

Who I Am

My name is Lindokuhle Mgoduka. I am a software development student, two-time Cum Laude graduate, SI leader, mentor, and tutor passionate about helping students succeed academically and professionally.


I created SEI Tutoring to provide a learning environment where students can ask questions freely, understand concepts deeply, and build real confidence in programming.

What Makes This Different

Many students struggle because programming explanations feel too complicated. At SEI Tutoring, concepts are broken down into simple, practical examples that make sense.


Sessions focus on:


  • C# Programming Fundamentals
  • Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
  • Database & SQL Concepts
  • Problem Solving & Logic
  • Exam Preparation
  • Assignments & Practical Support
