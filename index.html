About

Lindokuhle Mgoduka


I am a two-time Cum Laude graduate with a strong academic record, including a 90%+ pass mark in Software Development I. I have a solid understanding of programming fundamentals and a passion for helping other students succeed.


Over the past few years, I have worked as an SI Leader, Residence Tutor, and Peer-to-Peer Tutor, supporting many first-year students in challenging modules such as ONT1030/SDS1010.


My teaching approach focuses on simplifying complex concepts, using real-life examples, and guiding students step-by-step until they truly understand — not just memorise.


Through this platform, part of Small Empire Innovations (SEI), my goal is to create a supportive and student-friendly learning environment where students can build confidence, improve their skills, and successfully pass IT modules. I call it “a home of mistakes” — a space where students are encouraged to make mistakes, learn from them, and grow with confidence.
